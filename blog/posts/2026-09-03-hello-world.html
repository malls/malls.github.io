Hello world

Posted by Forrest on September 3, 2026 at 1:14 pm · Filed under: meta, writing · Permalink

This is the first post on this blog. It lives at forrestalmasi.com/blog, and it is where my writing gets published.

There is no build step here. Every page in this folder is hand-written HTML sharing one stylesheet, and publishing a post means editing a handful of files by hand: the post itself, the front page, the archive and the feed.

Nothing on this page phones anywhere else. No analytics, no trackers, no fonts fetched from a CDN, and no scripts at all — the page renders exactly the same with JavaScript switched off.

There is no comment system either. Replies go by email instead; the address is in the sidebar and at the foot of this post.

The feed is at feed.xml. It is RSS 2.0, it is updated by hand whenever a post goes up, and it is the way to follow this blog without checking the front page.

Every post is filed under one or more tags, and the archive lists everything by month and by tag. Right now there is one post, filed under two of them.
